The label is the control. Everything on this page is titled “campaign-linked contribution”. Incremental uplift and ROI are separate, later, differently-labelled things that require an approved methodology. A dashboard that quietly calls contribution “impact” is how an organisation ends up defending a number it cannot support.
Exclusions are stated on the figure that excludes them. Cost per verified attendee says “Bogura excluded” on the card itself, not in a footnote at the bottom of the page — because the card is what gets screenshotted into a deck.
