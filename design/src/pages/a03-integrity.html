“False positive” is a first-class outcome. Giving investigators an honest way to close a signal as wrong is what keeps thresholds tunable and stops the queue filling with noise people learn to ignore. A system that only permits “confirmed” or “open” will quietly train its users to leave everything open.
The access log is on the same page as the signals. The people investigating others are themselves visible here — including the supervisor's own evidence export. Oversight that exempts the overseers is not oversight.
