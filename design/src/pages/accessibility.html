Brand red is not a body-text colour. At 4.4:1 it misses the normal-text floor. It is legal on a filled button, as a 12px 600 micro-label on a tonal ground, and as a chart mark — never as a sentence.

Consent and purpose notice

Language choice before acceptance
✓Titled “Attendance photo and identity verification” — it says what happens, not “Terms”.
✓States ACSL, the campaign and session, the purpose, the data used, the provider category, retention, rights and contact, and what happens if the participant declines.
✓Optional marketing consent is a separate decision from attendance identity verification, and declining it does not block attendance.
✓Language is chosen before acceptance; the notice version, language, timestamp and outcome are all recorded.
✓Wording is non-coercive. There is a clear manual verification path where policy permits, and it is presented as a normal option rather than a failure route.
