Configuration is versioned, dated and reversible — and the interface says so. Every high-risk change carries a reason, an approver and an effective date, and the impact panel answers “what does this break” before the change is submitted rather than after it is discovered.
Restricted settings are listed but not shown. An admin who cannot see score bands is told they exist, who can see them, and what the business-facing equivalent is. Hiding the row entirely produces a support ticket; showing the raw threshold produces a security incident.
