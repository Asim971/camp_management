Colour is assigned by the job it does. Identity takes the categorical slots in fixed order. A sequence — funnel stages, match bands — takes the one-hue ordinal ramp so the reader sees the order in the colour. A series that means good or bad wears the status tokens. Nothing in this system is coloured by its rank, because that spends the identity channel re-encoding what bar length already shows.

Bands are a sequence, so they take the ordinal navy ramp. Outcomes mean good or bad, so they wear the reserved status tokens with an icon and a label. 47 records recommended “Low” were still approved by a reviewer — that divergence is the point of keeping the two bars apart, and it is a quality-audit signal, not an error.
Don't — one bar
Merging recommendation and decision into a single stacked bar makes the machine look like it decided 88% of cases. It did not decide any of them.
Don't — two y-axes
Never plot verified attendance and order quantity against two different scales on one chart. Any correlation the reader sees is an artefact of where the axes were anchored. Two charts, or index both to a common base.
