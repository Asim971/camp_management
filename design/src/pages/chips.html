Two objects, deliberately different

Status chip — reports state
⇡Pending sync ◍CRM review ✓Approved
Pill, tonal ground, icon then word. Not focusable, not clickable, not in the tab order. Announced as “Status: Approved”.
Filter chip — is a control
Territory SLA breach ✕ Savar ✕
Taller, bordered, dismissible. Focusable and clickable, with a visible active state and a removal affordance.
They must never converge. If a status chip gains a border and a hover state, users start clicking it and expecting a filter. Height, border and the trailing ✕ are the three signals that keep the two apart — none of them is decorative.

Don't

Don't invent vocabulary
⇡Syncing… ◍In review ✓Verified ✓
Three near-synonyms for controlled values. Now the mobile app, the CRM console and the dashboard describe the same record three different ways, and no one can reconcile a count.
Don't abbreviate to fit
⇡Pend. sync ⃠No ref.
If the chip does not fit, the column is too narrow or the table needs a horizontal scroll with a sticky identity column. The word does not get shortened.
