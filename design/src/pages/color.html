Surface and border

Application background is tinted, not white
surface.base #F8F9FC Application background. Cards sit on top of it
surface.elevated #FFFFFF Cards · dialogs · sheets · table body
surface.sunken #F1F3F9 Inset wells · chart tracks · skeletons
border.default #D9DDE8 Card, divider and field borders
border.strong #BFC5D6 Field hover · table header rule · unfilled marks
Why the background is tinted. Cards are elevation 0 with a hairline border, so a white page would leave nothing to distinguish a card edge from the page. The tint does the separating work that a drop shadow would otherwise do, which keeps dense tables calm.

Semantic state

All four ≥ 5.3:1 on an elevated surface
semantic.success #1F7A4D · 5.32:1 Approved · verified · completed. Reserved for outcomes, not activity
semantic.warning #B54708 · 5.43:1 Pending SLA · partial completion · needs attention
semantic.error #B42318 · 6.57:1 Rejected · failed · destructive action
semantic.info #175CD3 · 5.99:1 Processing · in review · informational
Brand red is not error red. An ordinary failure wears semantic.error, never brand.primary.600. If the two were interchangeable, a user could not tell a button they should press from a state they should worry about — and in this product the primary action often sits directly beside a rejection.

Ordinal — position in a sequence. One hue, monotone lightness

Giving each stage its own hue would say they are peers.

Usage rules

Do
One filled brand-red action. The alternatives step down in emphasis, so the eye lands on the action the page exists to perform.
Don't
Three filled buttons and two different reds. Nothing is primary, and destructive sits at the same weight as routine.
✓Filled brand red appears at most three times on a page: the primary action, the current navigation item, one principal chart series.
✓Status colour is always accompanied by an icon and a text label. Colour alone never communicates state.
✓Sensitive image areas use a neutral dark overlay. No promotional gradient appears anywhere inside CRM verification.
✓A series colour follows the entity, not its rank — filtering a chart never repaints the survivors.
