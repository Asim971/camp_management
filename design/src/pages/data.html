Every question a reader would raise is answered on the card: what it counts, out of what, how it was defined, where it came from, and how stale it is.
Don't — a vanity count
Total attendance 1,184
Attendance of what — captured, verified, or merely registered? Measured when? A number with no denominator and no definition will be quoted in a board pack and cannot be traced back.
Activity is not outcome. Registration and capture counts are activity. Verified attendance and canonical order quantity are outcomes. They are never placed in the same KPI row, because a reader scanning a row of four numbers will treat them as the same kind of thing.

Rules

✓Row order is action priority — exceptions and imminent dates first. Alphabetical is never the default for an operational queue.
✓Numeric columns are right-aligned with tabular figures, so magnitudes compare down the column.
✓Row click opens the detail; the overflow menu holds only permitted actions, and hides rather than disables the ones the role cannot perform.
✓In a verification context, a photo is a controlled evidence thumbnail — veiled, logged on open — never a decorative avatar.
✓A KPI card that is a rate always shows its denominator on the card, not in a tooltip.
