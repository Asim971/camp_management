Two rules govern everything on this page. Sensitive media is veiled until an authorised case is open, and every view is logged. The machine's output is a separate object from the human's decision, and the field user never sees a score at all.

The veil is a neutral dark overlay, never a blur — a blur invites the reader to squint at it, and a partially legible face is still a disclosure. Media is served through a short-lived signed viewer session. There is no permanent URL, download is disabled by default, and the session's remaining time is visible so a reviewer is never surprised by an expiry mid-decision.

Both frames share one aspect ratio, one crop and one zoom level. Zooming one zooms the other, so a reviewer is never comparing a face at two different scales — the single most common way a comparison produces a wrong answer. The reference is named, not implied. “Verified profile photo” and “Authorised NID photo” carry different weight in a decision, and a reviewer must know which one they are looking at.
