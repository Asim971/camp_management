Why this component exists. Two of the guideline's hardest requirements are about a record's position in a chain rather than its final state: capture success is not upload success, and the machine's opinion is not the human's decision. Both are easy to lose when a record is reduced to one status chip. So the chain itself is a component — and it renders identically on the mobile queue, the CRM case, Carpenter 360 and the audit trail, because a field operator and a reviewer arguing about the same record need to be looking at the same picture.

The rail

Six links · one direction · no shortcuts
  1. Captured10:24 · on device
  2. Queued10:24 · encrypted
  3. Uploaded10:31 · 1 retry
  4. Matched10:32 · advisory only
  5. CRM decision11:06 · S. Ahmed
  6. Countedincluded in verified
A completed chain. Every node names itself and carries its own timestamp, so the record can be reconstructed without opening the audit log.

A blocked machine step does not break the chain. The evidence is preserved and the decision moves to a human — the rail shows exactly that, rather than a dead end.
Returned for recapture — the chain loops back, and says so
  1. Capturedattempt 1 · 10:02
  2. Uploaded10:04
  3. Matchedlow quality
  4. Returned10:41 · poor quality
  5. Recaptureattempt 2 · assigned

The returned attempt stays in the record. A recapture is attempt 2, never a replacement of attempt 1 — the original evidence and the reason it was rejected both remain auditable.

Vertical variant

Narrow surfaces · same semantics
  1. Captured 10:24 · session 3 · device BMD-A21
  2. Uploaded 10:31 · after 1 retry
  3. CRM review assigned to S. Ahmed · 41m in queue
  4. Counted not yet included in verified attendance
The vertical rail is used on the mobile queue detail and inside the CRM audit panel, where there is room for a fuller caption per link. Nodes may be merged when a surface does not need the full chain — the mobile queue collapses Queued and Uploaded into one — but the order is never rearranged and a link is never skipped silently.

Rules

RuleReason
Never brand redRed is reserved for the primary action. The rail is a report, not a control, and must never look like something to press.
Dashed connector = not yetA future step and a failed step are different facts. Greying both out is the mistake this component exists to prevent.
Tone carries outcome, text carries identityColour-blind, greyscale and glare-readable by construction. Every node names itself.
Order is fixedThe chain is the audit story. Reordering it per screen would make two surfaces disagree about the same record.
An attempt is never overwrittenA recapture is attempt n+1. The returned attempt and its reason stay in the record.
Machine and human are separate links“Matched” is advisory. “CRM decision” is the decision. Collapsing them would imply the machine decided.
Announced as an ordered list. Each node reads “Uploaded, completed 10:31” or “CRM decision, not started”, so a screen-reader user gets the same distinction between a pending step and a failed one that a sighted user gets from the dashed connector.
