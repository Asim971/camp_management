Blocking and optional are different colours, different marks and different words. An operator who cannot tell them apart will either refuse to start when they could have, or start when they should not. Every optional row is labelled “Optional” in its own note text, not just tinted amber.
“Start attendance offline” names the situation. The primary action changes its own label to match the state it will operate in, so the operator's expectation is set before the first capture rather than after the first failed upload.
The roster is cached deliberately. A field session in Ashulia may have no usable network for three hours. Caching 236 registrations — names, IDs, phone suffixes, no photos — is what makes offline capture possible, and it is why the readiness check reports the cache as a first-class item.
