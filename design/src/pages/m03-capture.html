Consequence of refusal is stated plainly. “Your attendance will still be recorded” is the sentence that makes the consent real rather than performative. If declining the photo cost the participant their attendance, this would be coercion dressed as a choice.
Manual verification is an outlined button, not a text link. It sits at the same visual weight tier as a real alternative, because it is one.

Specification

DimensionSpecification
Sequence1 language-selectable purpose notice · 2 face positioning guide · 3 live camera · 4 quality result · 5 submit
GalleryDisabled. The photo must be taken in the moment.
DesktopA desktop capture mode is not recommended for normal field operation.
Primary actionsAccept and continue · capture · recapture · submit · use the manual route under policy
Required statesNo face · multiple faces · blur · poor light · wrong orientation · permission denied · storage full · offline queued
Never shownRaw match score, match band, reference image, or any judgement about the participant's identity
Colour discipline on the capture path. Normal framing guidance is neutral white on the dark viewport. A correctable problem is amber and still lets the operator capture. Red appears only when the device actually refused to save the photo. If framing hints were red, an operator would learn to ignore red — and then miss the one that mattered.
The quality screen reports on the photo, not on the person. “Lighting is uneven” is about the image. There is no band, no score and no reference photo on this screen, so the operator has nothing to repeat to the participant and no basis on which to form an opinion about them.
