Discard is deliberately awkward. It sits last, in text style, behind a bottom sheet that names the consequence, demands a reason, says the action is recorded, and states plainly that a queued upload almost never needs discarding. Every one of those is friction on purpose — a discarded capture is a participant who has to be photographed twice.
Per-item progress, not one global bar. A single “syncing 72%” bar tells an operator nothing about whether the specific person standing in front of them is done. Each row owns its own state, retry count and next attempt time.
