Why is a manual route needed? The participant's answer is recorded with the attendance record.
A bottom sheet keeps the thumb in the lower half of a 6-inch screen, which is the only part of the device an operator can reach while holding it steady. Options are full-width rows, not a picker wheel. The sheet is dismissible by dragging the grip, and dismissing always returns the user to exactly where they were — a field user interrupted mid-capture must never lose the carpenter they had selected.
Focus and dismissal. Opening moves focus into the sheet and traps it there; Escape or the grip closes it and returns focus to the control that opened it. The scrim is not the only way out.
