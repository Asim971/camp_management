Radius

Radius encodes the surface class
radius.1 · 6pxChips, compact controls
radius.2 · 8pxFields and buttons
radius.3 · 12pxStandard cards
radius.4 · 16pxDialogs and sheets
radius.5 · 24pxHero and cover
Radius is a signal, not a style preference: a 16px corner tells the user this surface is temporary and dismissible, a 12px corner says it is part of the page. Mixing them breaks that read.

Elevation

Tinted to the ink hue · never stacked
elevation.0Tables, inline sections, default surfaces
elevation.1Cards and sticky bars
elevation.2Menus, popovers, side sheets
elevation.3Dialogs only
Default to elevation 0. A dense operational page with shadowed cards reads as clutter. Separation comes from the hairline border against the tinted page. Shadow is reserved for surfaces that genuinely float above the page, and two floating surfaces never stack.

Grid and working width

12 columns · 24px gaps · 1440px maximum
Content is capped at 1440px and centred. Beyond that the gutter grows rather than the measure — a form field stretched to 1800px is unreadable and a table row becomes impossible to track across.

Responsive priority order. When space runs out, things are given up in this order: secondary metadata collapses into a disclosure, tables become cards or gain a sticky identity column, filters move into a sheet. Current status and the primary action are never what gets dropped — and sync, privacy and SLA warnings are never hidden just because the viewport is narrow.
