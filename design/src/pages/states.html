An error explains the correction and preserves the user's work. That single sentence decides most of this page. A field user who loses a capture because of a network failure will stop trusting the app, and a campaign admin who loses a half-built wizard will build it in a spreadsheet instead.

Loading

Skeletons shaped like the content · never a centred spinner
A skeleton in the shape of the result stops the layout jumping when data lands, and tells the user what kind of thing is coming. A bare spinner is acceptable only inside a button that is already labelled with the action.

Empty

Three different empties, three different next steps
▤
No campaigns in your scope yet Campaigns you own or approve will appear here. Create one to get started.
⚟
No campaigns match these filters 62 campaigns are in your scope. Try removing “Returned” or widening the date range.
✓
Your queue is clear Nothing is assigned to you. 31 unassigned cases are waiting, 6 of them nearing SLA.
An empty state that says only “No results” wastes the one moment the user is most willing to be told what to do. Each of these names the situation, gives the number that puts it in context, and offers the single most likely next action.

Failure and recovery

From §9.4 — every failure has a designed experience
Sales Eco profile API unavailable
⟳
Registration saved · profile confirmation pending The Sales Eco master is not responding. Your 3 registrations are held and will reconcile automatically. They show as Pending profile sync until then.
No local profile is created. There is no path here that produces a shadow master record.
Media upload failing
⇡
3 captures waiting to upload All 3 photos are saved and encrypted on this device. Retry runs automatically; next attempt in 4 minutes.
Nothing here suggests recapturing. Capture succeeded; only the upload has not.
Face provider unavailable
◍
Comparison delayed · evidence preserved Automatic comparison is unavailable. These 12 records are routed to manual review and keep their place in the SLA queue.
Concurrent CRM decision
⚠
S. Ahmed decided this case 40 seconds ago Approved, reason “Match confirmed”. Your draft reason was not submitted and is shown below in case you want to raise a quality-audit flag.
The case is version-checked on submit. The loser of the race is told who won and what they decided — never just “conflict”.
Order feed delayed
◷
Order data is 41 minutes behind Attendance figures are current. Order contribution and ROI below exclude orders placed after 09:01 and will rise when the feed catches up.
A delayed feed shows a delayed-data banner and the last refresh — never a misleading zero.
Notification delivery failed
⚙
Approval recorded · 1 notification not delivered The workflow is committed. The approver's email bounced; operations has been notified.
A failed notification never implies a failed workflow, and this detail is surfaced only to support and operations roles.

Permission and blocked

🔒
You don't have access to campaign approval Approval is limited to the Campaign Approver role, and an approver cannot approve a campaign they created. Ask an Org Admin if you believe this is wrong. Reference SEC-403 · your roles: Campaign Admin, Field User
⌖
Location permission is required This session enforces a venue geofence, so attendance capture needs location access. Open Settings → Apps → BMD Campaign → Permissions → Location.
⚠
Couldn't load this case The evidence viewer session expired. Reopening creates a new session and logs the access again — nothing about your queue position changes.
A permission-denied state names the rule, not just the refusal. “You don't have access” sends the user to a support ticket. “Approval is limited to the Approver role, and an approver cannot approve their own campaign” tells them whether to escalate or to ask a colleague — and the reference code makes the ticket short.
