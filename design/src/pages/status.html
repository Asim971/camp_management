One vocabulary, one renderer. These are the only status words the product may use. They are not re-worded per surface, not abbreviated in tables, and not translated into a synonym by a chart legend. If a new state is genuinely needed it is added here first — a screen may not invent one.

Integrity

Warning and error semantics · neutral language
⃠No reference ◌Poor quality ⚠Suspected spoof ⧉Duplicate ⌖Geofence exception ✎Manual override
Factual, neutral, non-accusatory. A failed liveness or match result means “Review required”, not “Fraud detected”. These labels describe what the system observed, never what it concluded about a person — the observation is defensible, the accusation is not, and a field operator reading the second one over a participant's shoulder is a real incident.
Do
⚠Suspected spoof Names the signal. A reviewer knows what to look at and the participant is not accused.
Don't
⚠Fraud detected · risk 0.87 A conclusion the system is not entitled to draw, plus a raw score with no approved, explainable policy behind it.
