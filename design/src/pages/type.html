Two treatments, chosen by job
Tabular — columns that must align
Registered1,184
Captured911
Verified1,107
Rejected76
Digits share one advance width, so the ones column lines up and a scanning eye can compare magnitudes without reading.
Proportional — a number as a headline
Verified attendance 1,107 of 1,184 registered · 93.5%
A standalone value reads better with natural spacing and tightened tracking. Tabular figures at 34px leave visible gaps around the 1s.

Noto Sans Bengali carries the Bangla. Inter has no Bengali coverage, so it is declared as the fallback at matching weight and line height. Bengali conjuncts and the matra line need the full 24px leading at 16px — do not tighten Bangla body copy to fit a layout. Where a label wraps to two lines in Bangla, the English layout has to tolerate two lines too, or the two versions stop being equivalent.
