Two deliberate inversions of the usual dashboard. First, the KPI row does not open the page — the action backlog does, because a campaign admin's morning question is “what is stuck”, not “what is the total”. Second, conversion and commercial outcome are two separate sections with their own headings, so no reader can scan one row of numbers and conclude that attendance produced sales.
Brand red appears three times on this page: the Create campaign button, the current navigation item, and the principal series in the product-mix chart. Nowhere else — the exception cards use semantic tones so that “needs action” never competes with “here is the button”.
