The overflow menu holds only permitted actions. An action the role cannot perform is absent, not greyed out — a disabled “Cancel campaign” invites a support ticket. The mobile card keeps name, date, location, status, target versus verified, and the next action. Type, owner, code and approval age move to the detail view.
