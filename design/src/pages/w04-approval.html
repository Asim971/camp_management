Acknowledge-to-enable, not warn-and-hope. Approve is disabled until the reviewer ticks each critical warning. This is the one place in the system where a disabled primary action is correct — the reason sits directly above it, the two safe outcomes stay available, and the acknowledgement itself is recorded, so an audit can show the approver saw the risk rather than assuming they did.
“Changed” is marked, not diffed. A full side-by-side diff of a campaign is unreadable. Marking the four changed fields inline, with a “Show only changes” filter, lets the approver re-review exactly what moved and trust that everything else is what they already accepted.
