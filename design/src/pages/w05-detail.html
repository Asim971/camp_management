Readiness is surfaced before activation, not at capture time. A field user standing in Ashulia discovering that the venue has no geofence has already lost the morning. The readiness panel names each check, separates blocking from optional, and puts the fix one click away for the person who can actually perform it.
The active session is the only raised card. Elevation is used here as a locator rather than decoration: on a campaign with eleven sessions, the one that is currently taking captures should be findable without reading a single date.
